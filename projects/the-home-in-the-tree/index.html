Educational Film

The Art Class

In the 1990s, in a rural village in Shaanxi, a local school temporarily hired an art teacher to meet inspection requirements. Unpredictably, his bold personality baffled the conservative leadership — but he transformed the lives of a group of students.

Director: Zhang Zhonghua   Cast: Wang Jianfang, Du Xuguang, Zhang Zimo   My Work: Lead Actress & Wardrobe Styling (Xue Ju)

During my middle school years, I had the opportunity to take part in this film, which first prompted me to reflect that education is not just about delivering knowledge, but also about provide an environment to effect how people think, reflect and make decisions.

Inspiration and Social Context

In rural China during the 1990s, an exam-oriented selection system concentrated educational resources on high-achieving students. Those who performed poorly and came from impoverished families were marginalized within the rigid academic hierarchy.

Plot Summary

This film tells a story about a rural middle school at 19th century in China. Several students were left to their own devices. However, a unique art teacher provides them an inclusive and vibrant environment.

Storyline and Character Introduction

This section introduces the key characters in The Art Class. It outlines their backgrounds, personalities, and motivations, highlighting how their individual journeys intersect with the themes of education, self-discovery, and personal growth within a rural school setting.

XUE Ju & JIA Miaohong

A city-born girl temporarily living in the countryside after her parents' divorce, academically gifted and disciplined. Her mother projecting her unfulfilled dreams onto Xue Ju. Yet Xue Ju is drawn to fashion design, seeking her own path between inherited expectations and personal aspiration.

JIA Miaohong

A well-known top student at Tuxing Middle School and head monitor of Class 4, Grade 9. Competitive, sharp-tongued, and unwilling to back down, she is entrusted by Xue Ju's mother to discreetly keep an eye on Xue Ju at school.

BA Wangchaoguo and his friends

Born into a rural family, BA Wangchaoguo struggles academically and is often overlooked. Passionate about drawing, he escapes to the riverbank to paint alone. With guidance from the art teacher, he gradually recognizes his artistic talent.

LENG Xuan — The Art Teacher

He was merely a temporary art teacher at Shaanxi's Tuxing Middle School, brought in to satisfy the education inspection. Wearing sunglasses and carrying a stoic yet rebellious aura, he stood out against the drab school environment.

Passionate about painting and singing, his art was misunderstood, his songs unappreciated. Yet, he defended the essence of education: it is not about selection, but about illuminating every mind.

"If we judge them solely by academic performance, they are what people call 'underachievers'... But when we look at these children from the perspective of life itself, I believe each of them is a genius, a unique individual."
