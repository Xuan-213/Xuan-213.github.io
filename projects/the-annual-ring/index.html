Unconscious Mirror

Drawing on Jung's idea that unseen cognitive and emotional patterns shape human behavior, this project explores how reflective awareness can support learning and leadership development.

InspirationBackground
During high school, I suffered from unconfidence. Later, during psychological counseling, I came into contact with psychology projective technique — an approach to understand subconscious cognitive patterns widely used in self or team development.

"People tend to leak subconscious content unconsciously or in a safe context." — Sigmund Freud

Through psychology projective technique, I learned to reshape subconscious cognitive patterns through reflective image association.

How can we recognize the psychological and behavioral patterns in our subconscious and make changes?

Design Goals

We cannot change what has happened in the past, but we can change our inner experience and interpretation of these memories.

01. Psychological Projection

AI guides users to interpret vague scenarios, leading them to gently re-enter scenes and understand restrictive beliefs.

02. Imagery Dialogue Technique

Introducing new resources supports the story, helping patients alter emotional perception and reduce pain, then change their behavior.

Worldview & Player Identity

Worldview

Humans are souls containing fragmented memory — restrictive beliefs from past lives. When all pain is released, transcendence is reached. If unable to escape scars, the soul repeats its experiences.

Player's Identity

The player is a soul repeatedly lost. Through countless cycles, always falling into the same fate.

Player's Goal

Find past self, activate memory, modify what cannot be reconciled. Rewrite destiny through interpretation.

Game mechanisms: Single-player & multiplayer

📖 Story System

Players either individually or in teams take turns to interpret the ambiguous scenes and characters stories. AI counselor analyzes stories, uncovering subconscious limiting beliefs.

⚔️ Combat System

Through battles, help characters gain courage and strength; Winning battles earns currencies such as stars, energy, and courage which can be used to purchase items and resources to change the direction of the story.

⭐ Economic System

Winning earns stars, energy, and courage to change the story's direction. These resources can be obtained by overcoming one's inner demons and can be used to purchase the necessary and optional sand toys.

🌱 Growth System

Player's soul upgrades as it rescues itself. Traumatic beliefs are healed. Players uncover their subconscious limiting beliefs by interpreting the stories. The AI counselor will analyze the stories told by the players.

🎬

🔍

✨

Reshaping

🕊️

Rescuing the character while changing the player's own self.

Outcome

Usability Testing

Exploring how open-ended stories and perspective shifts support reflective interaction.

Purpose
Compare AI-guided and written reflection, focusing on expression, examination of assumptions and the development of action strategies.
Participants
6 participants took part in the comparison.
Method
An interactive prototype was used to compare AI-guided reflection with written reflection.
Focus
Narrative expression, question comprehension, perspective shifting, reflection depth and the specificity of action strategies.
