SafeGuard

Addressing the rapid evolution of fraud tactics, this project explores a real-time, AI-assisted anti-fraud design for everyday use. By processing phone call transcriptions locally, the system identifies potential fraud patterns and delivers timely risk alerts.

Outcome

Family and friends can share safety statuses and anti-fraud insights. When high-risk interactions are detected, the system alerts designated contacts through multiple channels without exposing specific conversation details. This ensures immediate collective support while strictly safeguarding personal privacy.

SafeGuard performs real-time keyword detection and on-device risk analysis during social chats and calls to ensure user safety. While browsing, users can simply highlight any content to consult the AI regarding its security and credibility.

Users can grasp their daily security landscape and trace monthly records. Beyond keeping these as private personal archives, users can initiate "Family Sharing", syncing potential risks and anti-fraud insights with loved ones to build a collective digital defense within the family. The system can automatically generates structured anti-fraud case summaries for sharing. Users retain full editorial control, allowing them to redact or adjust sensitive information to ensure maximum privacy while alerting others.
